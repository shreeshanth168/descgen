How DescGen AI Works

DescGen AI uses artificial intelligence to turn a short product name and a few bullet-point features into a complete set of ready-to-use content for your online store. Instead of staring at a blank listing page, you fill in three simple fields and get back a full package: a marketplace-ready description, an SEO title and meta description, relevant keywords, social media captions for Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp, a set of frequently asked questions, and a clean specifications summary.

Each marketplace has its own conventions. Amazon shoppers respond to structured bullet points and clear benefit statements. Etsy buyers respond to a warmer, more personal, story-driven tone. Flipkart and Meesho listings in India tend to favor simple, direct language focused on value. Selecting a platform before you generate tailors the output to match what buyers on that specific marketplace expect to see.

The free tier includes three generations per day, which is enough to test the tool on a handful of real products before deciding whether it's useful for your catalog.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is DescGen AI really free?

Yes. The free tier includes three generations per day at no cost, with no account required.

Is the generated content accurate?

DescGen AI writes based on the details you provide, but like any AI writing tool it can occasionally add descriptive language you didn't specify. Always review generated content before publishing it to make sure every claim about your product is accurate.

Which marketplaces are supported?

DescGen AI currently offers tailored templates for Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, Flipkart, and Meesho, along with a general option for any other platform such as WooCommerce or your own store.

Can I edit the generated text?

Yes — every generated section is plain text you can copy and edit freely before publishing it anywhere.
