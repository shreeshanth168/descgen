About DescGen AI

DescGen AI is a free tool built to help small online sellers write better product descriptions, faster. If you sell on Etsy, Amazon, Shopify, Flipkart, Meesho, or your own store, writing fresh, persuasive copy for every new product is time-consuming — especially when you're running the business solo.

Why We Built This

Most sellers either reuse the same generic phrasing across every listing, or spend far more time writing descriptions than actually running their store. DescGen AI generates a full set of ready-to-use content — a marketplace listing, SEO title and meta description, keywords, social captions, FAQ, and specifications — from just a product name and a few features.

What Makes It Different

Unlike most AI writing tools that produce one generic block of text, DescGen AI tailors its output to the platform you're selling on. Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, Flipkart, and Meesho each have different buyer expectations and listing conventions, so the copy is generated accordingly.

Who It's For

DescGen AI is built for independent sellers, small business owners, and creators who list products online and want professional-sounding copy without hiring a copywriter for every item in their catalog.
