Etsy SEO: How to Write Descriptions That Actually Get Found

DescGen AI Blog · Marketplace Tips

Etsy's search engine weighs your title, tags, and description together — but the description does more than most sellers realize. It's not just sales copy; it's a second chance to include the exact phrases a buyer might search for, in a natural, readable way.

1. Open With a Sentence a Human Would Actually Say

Etsy shoppers browse for craftsmanship and story, not corporate copy. Start your description the way you'd describe the item to a friend — what it is, what makes it special, and who it's for — before moving into specifics.

2. Weave Keywords Into Natural Sentences

Etsy's algorithm reads your description for relevant terms, but keyword-stuffed sentences read poorly and can hurt conversion even if they help search placement slightly. Aim to naturally include the material, occasion, and style descriptors a buyer might search — e.g., "handmade walnut jewelry box, perfect for a bridesmaid gift" covers material, product type, and occasion in one readable line.

3. Tell the Making Story, Briefly

One of Etsy's biggest differentiators from Amazon or Flipkart is that buyers actively want to know it's handmade or small-batch. A short line about your process or materials — without turning it into a full essay — builds the trust that drives Etsy purchases specifically.

4. Answer Sizing and Care Questions Up Front

For items like jewelry, apparel, or home decor, unanswered practical questions are one of the top reasons buyers message sellers instead of purchasing immediately. Include dimensions, materials, and care instructions directly in the description to reduce friction.

5. Use the First 160 Characters Wisely

Etsy often truncates the visible description in search and mobile previews. Put your strongest, most keyword-relevant sentence first, since it may be the only part a browsing shopper sees before clicking in.

6. Keep Updating Seasonal Listings

Etsy rewards freshness signals. If you sell seasonal or gift-oriented items, revisiting and slightly updating your description ahead of relevant seasons (wedding season, holidays) can help visibility, not just relevance.

Writing warm, story-driven copy for every listing takes real time.
